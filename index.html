The small Turismo

$ 590

Structured architectural silhouette featuring our hand-buffed rolled top handles and custom light-gold galvanic hardware. Suede-lined interior with dual compartments and dedicated card slip pocket.

  • Dimensions: 26 cm W x 18 cm H x 11 cm D (10.2" x 7.1" x 4.3")
  • Handle Drop: 9 cm (3.5")
  • Shoulder Strap: Detachable & adjustable (48 - 56 cm drop)
  • Closure: Magnetic flap with concealed brass lock

100% full-grain box-calf leather sourced from a certified gold-rated Italian tannery in Tuscany. Smooth micro-grain finish resistant to light precipitation and day-to-day scuffs. Lined in plush organic lambskin nappa.

  • Exterior: Full-Grain Italian Calfskin
  • Interior: Nappa Lambskin & Italian Suede
  • Hardware: Solid Brass with 24k Gold Electroplating

To preserve the natural grain and luster of your leather, protect it from sustained moisture, direct radiator heat, and abrasive cosmetics. Gently wipe with the provided dry microfiber cloth and store inside the organic cotton dust bag when resting.
